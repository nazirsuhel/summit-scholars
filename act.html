ACT

Undergraduate applicants to US colleges. Accepted by virtually all US institutions as an alternative to the SAT.

Length~2 h core (+40 min optional Science)
Score range1–36 composite
DeliveryTest centre (paper) or online/digital in some regions

Test structure

SectionContentTime
English50 questions35 min
Math45 questions50 min
Reading36 questions40 min
Science (optional)40 questions40 min
Writing (optional)1 essay40 min

How it's scored

The composite score (1–36) averages English, Math and Reading. Since the ACT redesign, Science is optional and not counted in the composite. Always check the latest format as it has changed in recent years.

Skills tested

  • Fast, accurate grammar and rhetoric decisions
  • Pre-algebra through trigonometry, with a broader math range than the SAT
  • Reading speed across four passage types
  • Interpreting charts and experiments in Science

Preparation tips

  • Pace is the main challenge: practise with a strict timer
  • Learn the 10 most common grammar rules thoroughly
  • Decide whether to take Science based on target schools' policies
  • Mark answers on paper carefully; there is no penalty for guessing
