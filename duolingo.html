English language

Duolingo English Test

Applicants to the many universities (mainly in North America, but also worldwide) that accept it. Check each school: some do not.

Length~1 hour
Score range10–160
DeliveryOnline, at home

Test structure

SectionContentTime
Onboarding and ID checkIdentity, environment scan~10 min
Adaptive testRead & complete, listen & type, read aloud, write about image, etc.~45 min
Video interview and writing sampleUngraded, sent to schools~10 min

How it's scored

Scores are 10–160 in 5-point steps with sub-scores for Literacy, Comprehension, Conversation and Production. The test adapts to your level. Results typically arrive within 48 hours.

Skills tested

  • Spelling, vocabulary and sentence-level accuracy
  • Understanding short audio
  • Natural speaking and writing on open prompts
  • Staying calm under a webcam-monitored environment

Preparation tips

  • Check your webcam, mic and internet connection before test day
  • Quiet room, nothing else on the desk, and no interruptions
  • Read the instructions each time: question types change quickly
  • Take the official practice test to see the interface
