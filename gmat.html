How it's scored

The total score (205–805) combines all three sections equally. Each section is scored 60–90. There is no essay section. Scores are valid for five years.

Skills tested

  • Quant: arithmetic and algebra (no geometry)
  • Verbal: reading comprehension and critical reasoning
  • Data Insights: table analysis, graphics, multi-source reasoning, two-part analysis
  • Choosing your section order, and using the answer-review-and-edit feature

Preparation tips

  • Practise Data Insights early: it is the least familiar section for most candidates
  • Use the three allowed question bookmarks and edits strategically
  • Timing matters more than perfection; guess and move on after ~2 min
  • Choose your section order in practice tests to find what suits you
