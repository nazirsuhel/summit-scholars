Graduate school

GRE General Test

Applicants to graduate programmes in nearly every field, plus many business schools that accept it in place of the GMAT.

Length~1 h 58 min
Score range130–170 per section; Writing 0–6
DeliveryTest centre or at home

Test structure

SectionContentTime
Analytical Writing1 "Analyze an Issue" task30 min
Verbal Reasoning2 sections, 27 questions total41 min
Quantitative Reasoning2 sections, 27 questions total47 min

How it's scored

Verbal and Quant are each scored 130–170 in 1-point steps; Writing is 0–6 in half-points. Sections adapt: your performance on the first section of each measure determines the difficulty of the second.

Skills tested

  • Vocabulary in context, reading comprehension and text completion
  • Arithmetic, algebra, geometry and data interpretation (high-school level)
  • Quantitative comparison questions unique to the GRE
  • Building a clear, well-supported argumentative essay

Preparation tips

  • Learn high-frequency words in context, not from random lists
  • You can move freely within a section, so flag and return
  • Use the on-screen calculator wisely; don't over-rely on it
  • Time the essay: 5 min planning, 20 min writing, 5 min editing
