English language

IELTS

Applicants to universities in the UK, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, Ireland, and many in the US; also used for immigration. Choose Academic for study or General Training for work/migration.

Length~2 h 45 min
Score rangeBand 0–9 (overall and per skill)
DeliveryPaper, computer, or IELTS Online

Test structure

SectionContentTime
Listening4 sections, 40 questions30 min (+10 transfer on paper)
Reading3 passages, 40 questions60 min
Writing2 tasks (graph/letter + essay)60 min
SpeakingFace-to-face interview, 3 parts11–14 min

How it's scored

Each skill is scored in bands from 0 to 9, in half-band steps. The overall band is the average of the four. Results are typically available in 3–13 days depending on format.

Skills tested

  • Accent variety in Listening and following a recording only once
  • Skimming, scanning and matching headings in Reading
  • Task 1 data description and Task 2 essay structure
  • Fluency, coherence and natural pronunciation in Speaking

Preparation tips

  • Speaking is with a human examiner; practise talking aloud on any topic
  • Writing tasks are scored on four criteria, so learn the band descriptors
  • Task 2 is worth twice Task 1; budget ~40 min for it
  • Practise reading under time pressure with real past papers
