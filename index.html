Frequently asked questions

How early should I start preparing?

Most students need 8–12 weeks of consistent study for GRE, GMAT, SAT, ACT or LSAT (LSAT often takes longer), and 4–8 weeks for English tests, depending on starting level.

Which English test do universities prefer?

It varies by institution and country. IELTS and TOEFL are the most widely accepted; PTE and Duolingo are accepted by a growing number of schools. Check each programme's admissions page before choosing.

Do I need GRE or GMAT for my master's programme?

MBA and many business master's programmes use GMAT or GRE; most other graduate programmes use the GRE, and some have gone test-optional. Policies change yearly, so verify with each programme.

Can I retake a test?

Yes, all nine allow retakes but each has its own waiting periods, annual limits and score-reporting rules. See the test's official policy.

Are the details on this site official?

No. This is an independent preparation guide. Test makers update formats, scoring and fees, so treat our summaries as a starting point and confirm on the official sites.
