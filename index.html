Frequently asked questions

How early should I start preparing?

Most students need 8–12 weeks for GRE, GMAT, SAT or ACT (LSAT often takes longer) and 4–8 weeks for English tests, depending on starting level.

Which English test do universities prefer?

It varies by institution. IELTS and TOEFL are the most widely accepted; PTE and Duolingo are accepted by a growing number of schools. Check each programme's admissions page.

Do I need the GRE or GMAT?

MBA and many business master's programmes take GMAT or GRE; most other graduate programmes use the GRE, and some are test-optional. Policies change yearly, so verify with each programme.

Are these details official?

No. This is an independent guide. Test makers update formats, scoring and fees, so confirm on the official sites.
