Law school

LSAT

Applicants to law schools in the US, Canada and a growing number of other countries.

Length~2 h 20 min (+ separate Writing)
Score range120–180
DeliveryTest centre or remote, digital

Test structure

SectionContentTime
Logical Reasoning2 scored sections (~24–26 questions each)35 min each
Reading Comprehension1 scored section (27 questions)35 min
Unscored variable sectionExperimental35 min
LSAT WritingSeparate, taken online35 min

How it's scored

Scores range 120–180, based on the number of questions answered correctly. The Analytical Reasoning ("logic games") section was removed in August 2024. LSAT Writing is unscored but sent to schools.

Skills tested

  • Identifying argument structure, assumptions, flaws and strengthening/weakening evidence
  • Close reading of dense, multi-passage, comparative texts
  • Fast, consistent timing across a section of 25+ questions
  • Staying sharp across a long, mentally intense test

Preparation tips

  • Do untimed drills first, then move to 35-minute sections
  • Keep a wrong-answer journal, noting why the correct answer is right
  • Learn question-type patterns until they are automatic
  • Many students need 3–6 months; start early
