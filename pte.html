PTE Academic

Students applying to universities in Australia, the UK, New Zealand, Canada and the US; also used for visas in some countries.

Length~2 h
Score range10–90
DeliveryComputer, test centre

Test structure

SectionContentTime
Speaking & WritingRead aloud, repeat sentence, describe image, essay…54–67 min
ReadingFill-in-the-blanks, re-order paragraphs, MCQ29–30 min
ListeningSummarise spoken text, dictation, MCQ…30–43 min

How it's scored

Overall and communicative-skill scores are 10–90. The test is scored entirely by AI, and results are typically available within about two business days.

Skills tested

  • Integrated tasks where one skill feeds another (e.g. listening then writing)
  • Speaking clearly into a microphone with fluent delivery
  • Spelling-accurate dictation
  • Quick, accurate summarising

Preparation tips

  • Don't restart or pause when speaking; fluency counts more than perfection
  • Learn each task type's scoring rubric
  • Master templates for Describe Image and Summarise Written Text
  • Practise with headphones and a real mic setup
