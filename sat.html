Undergraduate

SAT (Digital)

Undergraduate applicants, especially to US colleges. Many universities in the UK, Canada, Australia, Asia and Europe also consider it.

Length~2 h 14 min
Score range400–1600 total; 200–800 per section
DeliveryDigital, at a test centre

Test structure

SectionContentTime
Reading & Writing2 modules, 27 questions each32 min each
Math2 modules, 22 questions each35 min each

How it's scored

Total score is 400–1600 (two sections, 200–800 each). The test is adaptive by module: your results in the first module of each section determine the difficulty of the second.

Skills tested

  • Short passages paired with one question each
  • Grammar, punctuation, rhetorical synthesis
  • Algebra, advanced math, problem solving and data analysis, geometry and trig
  • Built-in graphing calculator (Desmos) available on all Math questions

Preparation tips

  • Practise inside the official Bluebook app to learn the interface
  • Learn Desmos shortcuts to solve algebra visually
  • Many Math questions can be solved quicker by plugging in answers
  • Take 2–3 full adaptive practice tests before test day
