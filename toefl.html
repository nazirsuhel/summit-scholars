How it's scored

The TOEFL iBT was redesigned in January 2026: it is shorter, partly adaptive and now reports scores on a 1–6 band scale, with legacy 0–120 scores also shown for a transition period. Confirm exact formats and score conversion with ETS and your target schools.

Skills tested

  • Understanding academic lectures and campus conversations
  • Writing clear, evidence-based responses under time pressure
  • Speaking in short, structured responses to prompts
  • Reading mixed academic and everyday texts efficiently

Preparation tips

  • Check that each of your target schools accepts the new score scale
  • Practise with the official ETS materials for the new format
  • Take notes while listening, using shorthand you can read back
  • Record your Speaking answers and review them
